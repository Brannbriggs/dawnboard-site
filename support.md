# Daywall support

Need help? Email **branndonbriggs@gmail.com**. Please say which
iPad or iPhone model and iPadOS/iOS version you have (Settings › General › About).

Daywall has no accounts and no servers, so we cannot see your family's
information, and we will never ask for your Apple Account password.

---

## Setting up Daywall

The first time Daywall opens it asks two things, and takes under a minute:

1. **Who's setting this up?** Your name, whether you are Mom, Dad or
   Parent, and your family name (type "Smith" and tap **Use "The Smith
   Family"**). Say whether another parent lives with you, or tap **Just me
   for now**. The iPad is recorded as yours, which the 🆘 SOS screen uses
   (an iPad can't FaceTime the Apple ID it is signed in with).
2. **Your kids.** Type each kid's first name; each gets their own colour
   (tap the colour to change it). Tap **Add later** to skip.

Tap **✓ Done** and Daywall is ready. It then offers two extras, one at a
time, and you can skip either: **📍 Use this iPad's location** for the
weather, and **📅 Connect calendars**. Both are also in ⋯ › Admin later.

- **Changing it later:** ⋯ › Admin › Family profiles (names, Mom/Dad,
  colours), 🌍 Home & weather (your town), 📅 Calendars, and 🆘 SOS & this
  device (whose iPad this is). To walk through the welcome again: ⋯ › Admin ›
  Family profiles › **👋 Welcome setup**.
- **A parent PIN** is the last of the welcome's optional extras (4 to 8
  digits, typed twice). Skipped it? About a day later Admin offers it again,
  and you can set it any time in ⋯ › Admin › Admin PIN. With a PIN set,
  Admin, grown-up changes and answering an SOS ask for it.
- **FaceTime for SOS:** ⋯ › Admin › Family profiles › ✏️ Edit on a parent ›
  **FaceTime (phone or email)**. Daywall shows only that one is set and
  whether it is a phone number or an email, never the number itself.
- **Joining a family someone already set up?** Don't set up a new one:
  ask them to send you an invitation (see "Sharing Daywall with the other
  parent" below) and open it on your device.

## My calendars aren't showing

Daywall shows the calendars that are already on this iPad. Check these in
order:

1. **Calendar access.** iPad Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars ›
   Daywall must be set to **Full Access**. ("Add Events Only" is not enough,
   because Daywall needs to read events.)
2. **The calendar is on this iPad.** Open Apple's Calendar app on the iPad.
   If the events aren't there either, the account isn't set up on this
   iPad: add it under iPad Settings › Calendar › Accounts (on newer
   versions, Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts) and make sure
   its Calendars switch is on.
3. **Refresh Daywall's list.** In Daywall: ⋯ › Admin › 📅 Calendars ›
   **Refresh calendars**. New calendars appear there.
4. **The calendar is switched on in Daywall.** In the same section, check
   that the calendar is shown and assigned to the right person.

Each iPad reads its own calendars. If the other parent's calendar is only on
their phone, share it with you in the Calendar app (or add their calendar
account to the iPad) so it appears on the family iPad.

## Sharing Daywall with the other parent

Daywall keeps your family's devices in step through iCloud. Each person
uses their own Apple Account; there is no Daywall account.

1. Make sure each device is signed in to iCloud (Settings › your name).
2. On the iPad where you set Daywall up: ⋯ › Admin › ☁️ Family sync ›
   **👪 Invite the other parent**, and send the invitation (Messages or
   Mail).
3. They install Daywall on their iPhone or iPad, then open the
   invitation there. Daywall opens and joins your family.

Changes on one device show up on the others within a few moments when they
are online. ⋯ › Admin › ☁️ Family sync always says in words how it is
going: ✓ Synced, ↑ Waiting to send, ⏸ Off or ⚠ Problem.

Your family's data is kept in the iCloud of the parent who set Daywall
up, and counts toward their iCloud storage. If that storage is full,
sync stops for everyone and the Family sync card says so.

## I forgot the parent PIN

If you remember it and want to change or remove it: ⋯ › Admin › Admin PIN.
Type a new PIN and save, or save it empty to remove the lock.

If you have forgotten it, there is currently no way to reset it inside the
app. Email us and we will help as far as we can. Deleting and reinstalling
Daywall clears the PIN on that device, but **it also deletes everything
Daywall stored on that device**, and if iCloud sync is on, your family's
information (possibly including the PIN) comes back from iCloud.

The parent PIN keeps curious hands out of settings. It is not a security
feature, so do not reuse an important passcode for it.

## Where is my family's information kept?

- **On your device**, in Daywall's own storage.
- **In your own iCloud**, if sync is on, so your family's devices stay in
  step.

Nothing is sent to us, and we have no servers. Your location is not
tracked; only your approximate area, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to
Apple — and to the US National Weather Service for US towns — to get the
weather. Calendar events are read from
the iPad and never copied anywhere. The full
details are in our [privacy policy](privacy.html).

## Backing up and restoring your family's information

Family sync keeps a copy of your family's information in iCloud. A backup is
a second copy that **you** keep: it is the only copy if you don't use family
sync, and it protects you if the iCloud copy is ever deleted. Make one now
and then, and after any big change.

**Save a backup:** ⋯ › Admin › 💾 Backup › **Save a backup**. The iPad opens
its share sheet: choose **Save to Files** (iCloud Drive is a good place),
AirDrop it to another device, or mail it to yourself. The file is called
`Daywall backup <date>.json`.

What is in it: chores, stars, routines, habits, notes and drawings,
countdowns, memories and the app's settings, but **never the admin or
grown-up PINs**. Keep the file somewhere private all the same. The calendars and photos
chosen on the iPad are not in it; choose them again after a restore.

**Restore a backup:** ⋯ › Admin › 💾 Backup › **Restore from a backup**, then
pick the file. Daywall says the date the backup was made and asks before it
changes anything. Restoring **replaces** what is on the iPad now; it does not
merge. The iPad keeps the PINs it has now.

**Changed your mind?** Right after a restore, **Undo the last restore** puts
back what was there before. It works once, for the most recent restore.

If a restore says the file "is not a Daywall backup" or is from a newer
version, nothing was changed. Check you picked the right file, and update
Daywall if the backup came from a newer version. If it says a part of the
file "is damaged" or holds "documents this app does not write", nothing was
changed either: the file was edited or broken after it was saved. Try an
older backup.

## How do I delete all of Daywall's data?

1. **From iCloud** (do this first if sync is on): iPad Settings › tap your
   name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage (on some versions, "Manage
   Storage") › **Daywall** › **Delete Data**. This removes the iCloud copy
   only; each device keeps what it already has until step 2.

2. **From each device:** touch and hold the Daywall icon › Remove App ›
   **Delete App**. This removes everything Daywall stored on that device.
3. **Backups:** older iPad backups may still contain Daywall's data until
   they are replaced or deleted (iPad Settings › your name › iCloud ›
   Manage Account Storage › Backups).

There is nothing to delete on our side, because we never received it.

## The weather says to set a town

⋯ › Admin › 🌍 Home & weather, then either:

- **📍 Use this iPad's location.** The iPad asks "Allow Daywall to use your
  location?"; choose **Allow While Using App** (or Allow Once). Daywall
  asks for the iPad's *approximate* location once, saves it rounded to about
  1 km, and shows "📍 From this iPad (approximate)" beside the place. It does
  not follow the iPad around: the weather stays for that place until you
  tap the button again.
- **Search for your town:** type its name and tap the right result. The
  place then shows "🔎 Chosen town".

Weather needs an internet connection; if the connection drops, Daywall
keeps showing the last forecast it got.

If the weather says "the town's country is not known", the town was saved
by an earlier version: search for it again and tap it, so Daywall knows
which country it is in. (That only matters for US towns when Apple Weather is
unavailable: Daywall asks the US National Weather Service only for a town
it knows is in the US.)

## The weather is for the wrong place

The location is saved once, not tracked. If the iPad has moved (or the
approximate location picked a neighbouring town), go to ⋯ › Admin ›
🌍 Home & weather and tap **📍 Use this iPad's location** again, or search
for your town instead. Approximate location can be a few kilometres out;
for the forecast that rarely matters, and a typed town is exact.

## "Location access is off for Daywall"

Someone chose "Don't Allow" when the iPad asked, or Location Services are
off. iPad Settings › Privacy & Security › **Location Services**: make sure
the switch at the top is on, then tap **Daywall** and choose **While Using
the App**. Then tap 📍 Use this iPad's location again. Nothing else in
Daywall needs location: if you would rather not allow it, type your town
in the search box instead, and the weather works the same way.

If it says location is **restricted**, Screen Time (Settings › Screen Time ›
Content & Privacy Restrictions › Location Services) or a school or work
profile is blocking it; type your town instead.

## What does SOS do?

A kid taps 🆘 SOS on the wall iPad. The iPad shows big FaceTime buttons for the parents straight away, and (when family sync is on) the family's other devices can get a Time Sensitive notification. Delivery through iCloud is not guaranteed, and Focus or notification settings can hide it. SOS is not an emergency service and does not call 911. A parent taps "I've got it" to clear it everywhere sync reaches.

**The SOS button is on the family's iPad.** On an iPhone it is off at first, so it can't go off in a pocket; the phone still gets SOS notifications. To show it on one phone (a kid's, say): ⋯ › Admin › 🆘 SOS & this device.

**If family sync isn't set up (or this iPad isn't signed in to iCloud), an SOS stays on the iPad where it was tapped:** no phone is told, and the SOS screen says so. If two kids tap SOS at nearly the same time, both show (the newest on screen, both in the history).

**SOS is not an emergency service and does not call 911. It notifies your family's devices through iCloud, which Apple does not guarantee to deliver; a phone where Daywall was swiped closed may not be told. For an emergency, call your local emergency number.**

The parent whose Apple ID the wall iPad uses can't be FaceTimed from that iPad (FaceTime can't call itself). With family sync on and shared, their phone gets the notification instead; with sync off, the screen says so plainly: go and find them.

## The Daywall Family subscription

Daywall is free for 2 months, then **$4.99 a month or $39.99 a year**,
billed by Apple. You confirm it with Apple when the trial starts, and your
Apple Account is charged when the trial ends unless you cancel at least
24 hours before.

- **Where:** ⋯ › Admin › ⭐ Daywall Family. A grown-up check comes first.
- **One per family.** The device that set up the family subscribes; the
  other parent's phone, once it has joined through family sync, is covered
  too. Family Sharing also covers your Apple family group.
- **Cancel or change plan:** Settings › your name › **Subscriptions** ›
  Daywall, or **Manage or cancel** in the same Admin section (on the
  device that bought it).
- **New phone or reinstalled?** ⋯ › Admin › ⭐ Daywall Family › **Restore
  purchases**.
- **If it ends:** nothing is deleted. 🆘 SOS keeps working, and you can
  still see everything and make a backup. Adding or changing things, the
  weather, reminders and the games stop until you subscribe again, and
  then everything is as you left it.
- **Refunds** are handled by Apple: [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
